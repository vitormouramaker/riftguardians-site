Code of Conduct

Short rules, so the game is good for everyone — including children and teenagers, who play too. They are part of the Terms of Use.

1. Display name

Your name is shown to friends and rivals. It may not:

  • insult, threaten or discriminate (by race, colour, origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or any other reason);
  • contain sexual content, graphic violence, or promote drugs or crime;
  • impersonate another person, another player or someone from the game's team;
  • contain personal data (yours or someone else's), such as a phone number, address or social media profile;
  • advertise or share links.

Tip: do not use your real name.

2. Fair play

  • Do not use programs, modifications or tricks to gain an advantage, to play in your place in PvP or to deceive the server.
  • Found a bug? Do not exploit it: tell us.
  • Do not attack, overload or probe the server.
  • Do not buy, sell or lend accounts.

3. Respect in PvP

  • Play the match to the end. Abandoning on purpose spoils the other player's game and earns you a waiting time in the queue.
  • Do not fix results or throw matches.
  • Pauses are for real emergencies. Do not ask for a pause just to disrupt your rival.
  • Emotes are for fun and for saying hello. Do not use them to taunt non-stop.
  • Do not create new accounts to escape a penalty or to prey on beginners.

4. What happens to those who break the rules

Depending on how serious and how repeated it is:

  1. the name is replaced with a neutral one;
  2. the account loses the online modes for a while;
  3. progress or rewards obtained by cheating are undone;
  4. the account is closed.

Abandoning PvP matches triggers an automatic waiting time. Everything else is decided by a person, and you can ask for a review through the contact address.

5. How to report

Write to contato@riftguardians.tech or use Discord, with the player's friend code or name, what happened and, if you can, a screenshot. Do not expose the person in public.

If someone is in danger or a crime is involved, contact the authorities. In Brazil, Disque 100 (dial 100) takes reports of violations of the rights of children and teenagers.
