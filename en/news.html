Version 0.9.0

Date: October 12, 2026

The first version distributed for testing. What it brings:

  • The full campaign: 10 maps, 100 stages and 10 bosses, with Heroic mode and the Arsenal.
  • 10 playable commanders, each with abilities, talents and a skill tree.
  • Guardians with shop, reserve, merging, faction synergies and summoning.
  • An online account created on your own computer, with no e-mail and no password. The campaign works offline and progress is sent when the connection returns.
  • Account protection: a recovery code, so you do not lose the account if the computer is wiped or lost, and a transfer code, to take the account to another computer (see Support).
  • Online PvP: Rift Duel and Commander Arena, through the queue or against friends, with a way back into the match after a disconnect, pause by agreement and emotes.
  • Friends and in-game mail.
  • Portuguese and English.
  • In-game notices for new versions and maintenance.

What does not exist yet in this version:

  • A digital signature: Windows shows a warning when you open the game (read why).

The game is in testing. Balance will still change, and progress may be reset during the testing period — if that is going to happen, we will announce it beforehand here and in the game.
